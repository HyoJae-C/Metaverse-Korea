Chat and reports. Chat (public chat and friend messages) is kept on our server for 90 days so that we can check reports of abuse; it is deleted automatically after that. Chat is open to everyone for now; we may later allow only players signed in with Google to send chat (guests could still read it). Reports you make in the game are kept on your device and in your save; they are not sent to us for review. To report someone to us, email yozi999a@gmail.com with their nickname, the time and what happened. The operator can read everything players write to each other in the game (public chat, friend messages, board posts and love-lock notes) to check reports of abuse and stop abusive players. Voice in the live café goes directly between players and is not recorded.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number or address, and we don't sell personal data. Purchases in the game are virtual only: there are no real payments. Practice voice recordings in the restaurant stay on your device. They are never uploaded.

Ads. The "Watch an ad" button for coins is optional. The game loads Google's ad script (AdSense / H5 Games Ads) so an ad is ready, and shows an ad only when you press the button. Google may use cookies or similar technology and your IP address to show and measure ads, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent first. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

3. Service providers

Supabase (accounts, saves, realtime), Google (sign-in, only if you choose it; ads for the optional "Watch an ad" button) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Their servers may be outside your country, including the United States.

4. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at yozi999a@gmail.com. Clearing your browser data removes the copy on your device. A guest account can't be recovered after that unless it was linked to a login.

5. Children

The service is not directed at children under 14. If you are under 14, please use it with a parent or guardian.
