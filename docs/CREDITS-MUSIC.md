# Music credits

Nothing in the Arts Center's halls is a recording. The game plays written
notes live (`src/core/concert-player.js`). Every work is long out of
copyright.

## Concert Hall (`src/data/concert-scores.js`)

The notes come from editions typeset in LilyPond by volunteers of the
[Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org) and converted with
`lilypond` (see `scripts/build-concert-scores.mjs`; the converted MIDI files
are in `scripts/data/scores/`). Each is a short excerpt, set for the game's
small orchestra or its piano.

## Opera Theater (`src/data/opera-scores.js`)

Written by hand for the game from the published scores: Tchaikovsky, Swan
Lake (1877); Verdi, Rigoletto (1851); Wagner, Die Walküre (1856); Offenbach,
Orpheus in the Underworld (1858).
